// about

I'm a computer science graduate student and builder. I like understanding systems all the way down — from the byte layout of a cipher to the way a packet moves through a socket — and then turning that understanding into tools other people can actually use.

My work sits between security research and practical engineering: C libraries, packet capture, port scanning, Telegram automation, and a master's thesis on improving the Ascon authenticated cipher for constrained IoT devices with combinatorial methods.

Most of the time I write the thing from scratch, keep the interfaces small, and test the parts that are easy to get subtly wrong.
